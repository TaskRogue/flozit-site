How it works

  1. Step 1

    Tag the leads you want worked in Follow Up Boss.

  2. Step 2

    Alex emails them from your Gmail. Every email says Alex is an AI assistant.

  3. Step 3

    When a lead is ready, you get a handoff brief and a call task in Follow Up Boss.

What you get

When a lead is ready, a handoff brief lands in your inbox and a call task appears in Follow Up Boss.

  • The lead's own words, so you know what they want before you call.
  • Why they are ready, in one line.
  • A call task already waiting in Follow Up Boss.

You stay in control

  • You pick every lead. Alex only emails people you tag in Follow Up Boss.
  • Every email says Alex is an AI assistant.
  • Anyone who asks to stop is never emailed by Flozit again.
  • Turn Flozit off any time.

How Flozit uses your Gmail

Flozit sends the emails you authorize, reads replies from your leads, and checks your emails to those leads so it never emails someone you are already talking to. It does not read or store your other email, and your data is never used to train AI models. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Questions

Will my leads know Alex is an AI assistant?

Yes. Every email says so.

Whose email does it send from?

Your own Gmail, so replies land in your inbox like any other email.

Can I see what Alex sends?

Yes. Every email is in your Gmail Sent folder. During the beta, a person on our team also reviews each email before it goes out.

What happens when a lead is ready to talk?

You get a handoff brief by email and a call task in Follow Up Boss. Alex stops emailing that lead so you can take it from there.

What if a lead asks to stop?

Flozit never emails them again.

Do I need to change how I use Follow Up Boss?

No. You add one tag, Flozit On, to the leads you want worked.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta.
