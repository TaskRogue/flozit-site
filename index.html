What it replaces

The hours you spend chasing homeowners for selections and signatures.

  • Texting photos of tile options and waiting days for an answer

    One link with the options, prices and a pick-by date, and reminders that go out for you

  • Printing a change order and driving it over to get it signed

    A change order signed from their phone with an emailed code, filed as a PDF

  • Calls from buyers 800 miles away asking how the house is going

    A weekly update written from your daily logs, and a page they can check any time

Everything a small builder needs, nothing you pay for and skip

Six parts that work together on one job page and one homeowner portal.

  • Selections

    Send 2 to 4 options with photos and prices. Homeowners pick from their phone, see where each option sits against its allowance, and get reminded until the pick-by date.

  • Change orders with e-signature

    Write the change, price each line and send it. Homeowners sign with an emailed code, and you get a signed PDF with the time and the signer on record.

  • Schedule

    Bring your plan from a spreadsheet, a PDF or a photo. Drag a step on the Gantt and see what shifts before anyone is told the move-in date changed.

  • Daily logs and weekly updates

    Log the day with notes and photos from the site. Friday, Flozit drafts the weekly update from your logs, and nothing sends until you approve it.

  • Homeowner portal

    One page per job with your name and logo on top: the move-in date, what they owe a decision on, approved changes and photos by week.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add the job and bring your plan

    Bring your schedule from a spreadsheet, a PDF or a photo. Flozit drafts it; you check it and save.

  2. 2

    Invite your homeowners

    They get their own link by email. No password, no app to download. They pick, sign and follow the build from their phone.

  3. 3

    Flozit chases the decisions

    Picks, signatures and tasks get reminders on the dates you set, from your name. You see who owes what on one screen.
