The cost of chasing

What chasing costs you

A late tile pick holds the tile crew and every trade booked behind them.

An unsigned change order is work you can’t bill yet.

A pick made from a texted photo turns into an argument at the walkthrough.

Every how’s it going text at 9 pm takes an evening you meant to have off.

What changes

What Flozit does about it

Selections in on time.

Homeowners see two to four options, each with its price and what it does to their allowance, and pick by the date you set. Reminders go out until they pick.

Change orders signed from their phone.

They read the price and the new contract total, then sign with an emailed code. The signed PDF is filed on the job.

The 9 pm question already answered.

Their page shows this week’s photos, what needs them, and the move-in date.

One list of what’s waiting on you.

Needs You shows every pick, signature and reply across all your homes.

Tip: Have a late selection fee in your contract? Flozit shows it beside every pick-by date, and drafts the fee for you to review when a pick runs late.

Questions builders ask

My homeowners won’t use another app.

There is no app. A link in your email opens their page on any phone. No password, nothing to install.

I already use Buildertrend.

Keep it for estimating and job costing. Flozit does the homeowner side: selections, signed change orders, the weekly update and the move-in date. Your schedule comes in from a spreadsheet, a PDF or a photo.

I don’t have time to set it up.

One sitting: [CONFIRM] minutes in five short steps, and you can stop and come back. During the beta, setup is done with you.

Where do homeowner replies go?

Homeowner replies show in Needs You, and Flozit emails you a copy.

Do I need to connect Gmail?

No. Emails go out from a Flozit address under your name and business. Connect Gmail if you want them sent from your own address.
