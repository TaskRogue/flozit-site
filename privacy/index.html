What Flozit collects

Flozit is software for home builders, run by Flozit LLC, a Tennessee company in Piney Flats, Tennessee ("Flozit", "we", "us"). You, the builder, use it to run your jobs with your homeowners. Your homeowners deal with your business. Flozit works for you behind the scenes. This policy covers the Flozit Builder beta.

About you, the builder.

  • Your account: your email address, your name, your business name, phone number, business mailing address, time zone and logo.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled hash (Argon2id). We can't read it.
  • When you set a password, we check it against a public list of leaked passwords (Pwned Passwords). Only the first 5 characters of a scrambled version of it are sent, never the password.
  • Sign-in records: the emailed sign-in codes, and failed tries counted by a scrambled form of your address and browser, so we can slow down anyone guessing.
  • What you agreed to at sign-up: each box, its version, its exact words and when you checked it.

Your work in Flozit: your jobs and their addresses, your homeowners, schedules, rooms, selections and their options, allowances, change orders, daily logs and photos, files, homeowner tasks, weekly updates, and the emails Flozit sends for you. Photos are cleaned when you upload them: location, camera and time data are removed.

Your Gmail, only if you connect it: permission to send email as you, and your Gmail address. Flozit can't read, change or delete anything in your Gmail. The access key Google gives us is stored encrypted.

Feedback you send: what you write (up to 5,000 characters) and any screenshot you add (PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 10 MB). With it we keep the page you were on, your screen size, the app version, your account ID and the ID of the last error report, if there was one. Screenshots are re-saved when they arrive, which removes their hidden data. You can send up to 20 a day.

How you use the app: error reports and, during the beta, usage analytics on builder screens. See Cookies and tracking.

What it is used for

We use your data to run Flozit for you, and your plans as described below:

  • to show your jobs, schedules, selections and change orders to you, and the right parts to your homeowners;
  • to send the emails you set up or approve, and the sign-in emails you ask for;
  • to sort homeowner replies, record a homeowner's pick, and pass you their questions;
  • to add the day's weather to a daily log: the job's address goes to the US Census Bureau's address lookup and the National Weather Service, both free US government services;
  • to read a supplier's product page when you paste its link, so an option starts filled in;
  • to help you when you ask, to fix problems, and to keep Flozit secure.

Survey answers you give, and how you use Flozit, are used to improve Flozit and to tailor your experience, for example which step we suggest first.

Where AI is used. Flozit uses Anthropic's Claude models for these jobs, and no others:

  • Sorting homeowner replies. When a homeowner replies to an email, the reply and the names of the open selections go to Claude, which says whether it is a pick, a question or something else. A clear pick is recorded. Anything else goes to you. The AI never writes back to a homeowner.
  • Drafting weekly updates. Claude drafts each weekly update from the week's facts in your daily logs and schedule. A second check looks for anything your records don't say. You approve, edit or skip every draft. Nothing is sent on its own.
  • Reading product links. When a supplier's page doesn't list its details clearly, or you paste its text or a screenshot, Claude reads it to fill in the option. You confirm every price.
  • Reading your schedule. If you upload your own plan (a spreadsheet, PDF, photo or pasted text), Claude reads it into a draft schedule for you to check.
  • Reading house plans. Plans and related files you upload to Files may go to Claude to read their rooms, sizes, labels and other plan details. We use this to build your 3D walkthrough, once 3D walkthroughs are offered, and to test and improve Flozit's features, including 3D walkthroughs. Your plans are never sold, never shared with anyone else, and never shown to other builders. You can opt out of the testing and improvement use (see Your choices).
  • Sorting feedback. A small Claude model sorts what you send into one of Bug, Confusing, Missing feature, Wording or Praise. It is used only to sort. It never acts on what you wrote.

We don't use your data or your homeowners' data to train AI models.

Flozit staff. Flozit staff may read your jobs and the emails in your account to help you and to fix problems, as you agreed at sign-up. Each time staff open your account to view it, it is recorded in an audit log. Viewing can't change anything or send anything. Unless you opted out, staff may also open and download the plans you upload, to test and improve Flozit. Each download is recorded in the audit log.

We never sell your data or your homeowners' data, and there are no ads in Flozit.

Homeowner data

You add your homeowners to your jobs, and you are responsible for your relationship with them. Flozit handles their data for you, to run the jobs you set up.

What we hold about a homeowner.

  • Their name and email address, and a phone number if you add one.
  • Their picks, with the time and a short device summary such as "Chrome on Windows". For a pick made by email reply, it says "By email". The full browser details and IP address are not kept for a pick.
  • When they first and last opened each selection and change order, with a scrambled record of the devices used, never the device's address.
  • Their e‑signatures and the change orders they declined, with any reason they gave. See Electronic signatures in the terms.
  • Files they upload for a homeowner task, and change requests they send.
  • Their email replies to you.
  • Whether they asked to stop emails.

Signing a change order. A homeowner signs with a six-digit code emailed to them, the consent box ("I agree to sign this change order electronically with a code sent to my email") and their typed name. The signed record keeps the typed name, the consent words and their version, the time, the IP address and browser used, the address the code was sent to, the change order's revision, a record ID and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the exact content signed. The code itself is stored only as a scrambled hash and is never shown on the PDF. The time is shown in the job's time zone, with the zone named, for example "Nov 19, 2026, 2:06 PM EST".

Homeowner pages. Homeowners never make an account. The link in their email is their access. A link works for 30 days from when it was last emailed. Opening it signs in that device, which stays signed in until 90 days after the last visit. Homeowner pages have no analytics and no error tracker in the browser.

Report a problem. A homeowner's Report a problem note goes to Flozit support, never to you. It keeps the job's ID and the page, not the homeowner's address. Up to 5 a day per link.

A homeowner who wants their data deleted can ask you, or email support@flozit.com. We delete what we hold about them. Signatures and the change orders they signed or declined are legal records, so we keep those unless you and the law allow us to delete them, and we tell them what we kept. Their address goes on your do-not-email list as a scrambled code, so Flozit never emails them for you again.

Email and Gmail

How your emails go out. Every homeowner email comes from your business. The From name is your first name at your business, like "Mike at Smith Builders". Flozit sends it one of two ways:

  • Flozit's sender (the default): through Postmark, an email service, from updates.flozit.com.
  • Your Gmail, if you connect it: from your own address, using send-only access. It shows in your Sent folder.

Every homeowner email ends with your business name and address, then "Written by [your business]'s AI assistant. Sent with Flozit." Reminders and weekly updates carry a link to stop them.

Replies. A homeowner's reply goes to a Flozit reply address (on reply.flozit.com), not your inbox. It reaches Flozit through Postmark, an email service. Flozit reads it, sorts it (see Where AI is used), shows it in Needs You and emails you a copy.

Emails to you. Alerts, copies of homeowner replies and draft reminders come from Flozit's own mailbox on Google Workspace. Sign-in codes and other account emails (the welcome email, password and email address changes) and the note that something you reported is fixed come through Postmark.

What we keep of an email. The full text of an email to a homeowner is kept only until it is sent. After that we keep its subject, when it went, who it went to and whether it arrived. Flozit's own copies of the alerts it emails you are deleted within about 4 months.

Turning Gmail off. Remove Flozit's access from your Google Account at any time. Flozit then stops sending from your Gmail.

Cookies and tracking

Cookies. Flozit uses only the cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, one that keeps a homeowner's device signed in to their page, and a few small ones that remember a choice, such as your last schedule view or an Undo. No advertising cookies. The flozit.com website has no cookies, trackers or analytics.

Error reports (Sentry). When something breaks, Flozit sends an error report to Sentry, an error tracking service. Before a report leaves Flozit, it is cleaned: no request bodies, cookies or sign-in headers, and email addresses, phone numbers, homeowner links and named personal fields are removed. A report that can't be cleaned is not sent. You are identified only by your account ID. Sentry is set not to store IP addresses. In the browser, the error tracker runs only on builder screens, never on homeowner pages, sign-in, signing or private files.

Usage analytics (PostHog), during the beta. To find what is confusing, Flozit records how builder screens are used with PostHog, an analytics service:

  • Builder screens only, once you finish signing up. Never homeowner pages, sign-in, password, signing, admin or private file pages.
  • No cookies and no browser storage. You are known by your account ID only, never your name or email.
  • Everything goes through Flozit's own server first, so PostHog never sees your IP address. Page addresses are sent without their search or private parts, and emails, phone numbers and homeowner links are removed from what is sent.
  • Session recordings, when on, hide everything you type, all homeowner details and every photo, and never record network traffic or the console.
  • You can turn recordings off in Settings, under The beta: Share usage recordings.
  • Flozit's own accounts and test accounts are never tracked.

Where data lives and for how long

Where. Your data is stored with Supabase (the database and private file storage) in US East (Ohio), in the United States. The app runs on Railway in US East (Virginia), in the United States. Access keys to other services are stored encrypted.

Who helps us run Flozit. Each sees only what its job needs:

  • Supabase: database and file storage.
  • Railway: hosting.
  • Google: Gmail sending if you connect it, and Flozit's own mailboxes.
  • Postmark: sending email and receiving replies.
  • Anthropic: the AI jobs listed in What it is used for.
  • Sentry: error reports.
  • PostHog: usage analytics on builder screens, during the beta.
  • The US Census Bureau and the National Weather Service: the weather on daily logs.
  • Pwned Passwords: the leaked-password check.

How long. Your jobs, homeowners, signed change orders, signatures and files are kept until you ask us to delete them, subject to legal requirements. Closing a job or cancelling your account deletes nothing. These are cleared or deleted sooner:

  • The full text of an email to a homeowner: once it is sent.
  • Words a homeowner wrote (replies, change requests, reasons for declining) and your on-site notes for a change order are cleared no later than 90 days after the job closes (or 90 days after they were saved when there is no job), and never kept longer than one year. The pick, the signature and the record of what happened stay.
  • A financing letter is deleted 30 days after you approve it.
  • Flozit's own copies of the alerts it emails you are deleted within about 4 months.
  • Feedback: the text of a report is cleared a year after it is sent, and its screenshots are deleted 90 days after we close the report.
  • A schedule file you upload to bring in your plan: when you save the schedule or start over, or when its draft expires.
  • Copies of your plans made to test Flozit: deleted when you delete the plans or opt out.
  • Sign-in codes, signing codes and records of failed tries: automatically, after a short time.

Your choices

Builders.

  • Change your details in Settings.
  • Turn off Share usage recordings in Settings.
  • Turn Automatic emails off in Settings, so nothing goes to homeowners until you turn them back on.
  • Disconnect Gmail from your Google Account at any time.
  • Download a closed job's records from its Downloads: the closeout record, the signed change orders and the photos.
  • Ask for a copy of what we hold, or for corrections, at support@flozit.com.
  • Opt out of plan testing: email support@flozit.com from your account's address. Your plans are then excluded from testing and improvement, and test copies deleted. We still use them to build your own walkthroughs once they are offered.
  • Cancel: email support@flozit.com from your account's address. We stop sending anything for you, remove Flozit's access to your Gmail, delete your password and end every session. Nothing else is deleted: your records are kept until you ask us to delete them, subject to legal requirements. For now a cancelled account can't sign in, so download what you need first.
  • Delete your data: email support@flozit.com from your account's address. We confirm the request is yours, then delete your data. Signed change orders, signatures and the records of closed jobs are legal records: we tell you which are held before we delete them. We keep the record of what you agreed to at sign-up, and a scrambled code for each homeowner who asked to stop emails, so they are never emailed again.

Homeowners: use the stop link in any reminder or weekly update, ask your builder, or email support@flozit.com to see or delete what Flozit holds about you.
