1. What Flozit does

Flozit helps real estate agents follow up with their existing leads by email. You choose leads by adding the Flozit tag to them in your Follow Up Boss account. In these terms, to tag a lead means to add that tag. An AI assistant named Alex emails those leads from your own Gmail account and may reply to their answers. When a lead seems ready to talk or asks for you, Flozit hands the lead back to you. It emails you a short summary called a handoff brief, and adds a note and a call task in Follow Up Boss. Alex may send the lead one last short reply, then stops emailing that lead.

Flozit contacts leads only by email. It does not call or text leads, set appointments or negotiate for you.
