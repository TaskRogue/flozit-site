Homeowner pages

Each homeowner gets their own page for your job, with your business's name first and a small "Powered by Flozit" line at the bottom. Homeowners never make an account: the link in their email is their access. Anyone with the link can open the page, so a forwarded link is recorded on the job, and signing still needs a code sent to the homeowner's email.

You can turn a homeowner's link off, or remove a homeowner, at any time. A homeowner can stop reminders and weekly updates from the link in those emails. Flozit then sends them no more of those.

A homeowner's Report a problem note goes to Flozit support, not to you.

Electronic signatures

Homeowners can sign a change order online. To sign, a homeowner:

  1. opens the change order from their email;
  2. checks the consent box: "I agree to sign this change order electronically with a code sent to my email";
  3. enters the six-digit code emailed to them, which works for 10 minutes, once, with 5 tries;
  4. types their name.

The signed record keeps the typed name, the consent words and their version, the time, the IP address and browser used, the address the code was sent to, the revision signed, a record ID and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the exact content signed. A signature can't be edited. The change order's PDF shows the signer's name, "Signed with a one-time code sent to" their address, the time in the job's time zone with the zone named (for example "Nov 19, 2026, 2:06 PM EST"), the agreement version, the record ID and the fingerprint. It never shows the code.

To change a sent change order, you send a new revision under the same number, and the old one becomes Void. History is kept. A change order you mark as signed by hand is recorded as your entry, with your reason, never as the homeowner's e‑signature.

It is up to you that e‑signing works for your contracts and your state. Flozit doesn't give legal advice.
