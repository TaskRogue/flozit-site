A note from me

I built Flozit for builders who would rather be on site than on the phone. Four to ten homes a year, buyers who often live out of state, and evenings spent answering texts about things the homeowner could have seen for themselves.

Flozit gives each home one page with your name on it. Homeowners pick, sign and follow the build there. You approve, and Flozit does the chasing.

During the beta your notes come straight to me. If something is confusing or missing, tell me.
